Selling a file to a stranger in another country is three problems wearing one
coat: taking the money, delivering the file, and proving to two tax authorities
that you did both properly.

This book is about the third one, because the first two are solved products and
the third is where people quietly get it wrong for two years and then receive a
letter.

### What is in it

* Place of supply for digital services, in plain sentences, with the two pieces
  of evidence you are required to keep and what counts as contradictory.
* Why the reverse charge is not a favour you do a business customer, and what
  your invoice has to say when it applies.
* Invoice numbering without gaps, and why a gap is a question you will have to
  answer years later.
* Refunds, credit notes, and the difference between cancelling a sale and
  correcting one.
* What to keep, where, and for how long — with the retention periods for Poland,
  Germany and the UK side by side.

### Who it is for

Anyone selling their own ebooks, courses, presets, templates or plugins
directly. If a platform handles the tax for you today, the last chapter is about
what changes on the day you decide it should not.
