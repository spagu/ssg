These terms are a starting point for a shop selling digital books to consumers
in the EU and the UK. They are not legal advice: have them read by someone whose
job that is before you take real money.

VAT PL0000000000. Written questions: help@example.com.

## What you are buying

A digital file — a PDF and an EPUB of the same book — delivered as a download
link by email. Nothing is posted to you, and there is nothing to return.

## Price and tax

Prices on the site include VAT at the rate of the country you give at checkout.
That country decides the rate, and your payment details have to agree with it:
this is a rule of the tax, not of this shop. Buying with a valid business VAT
number from another EU country moves the tax to you under the reverse charge,
and the invoice says so.

## Delivery

The link is emailed as soon as the payment provider confirms the payment —
normally within seconds. It works for a limited number of downloads over a
limited period, both shown on the link page. If you lose the email or the link
expires, [ask for it again](/shop/resend/); that is not a favour, it is part of
what you bought.

## Your right to withdraw, and why we ask you to waive it

For digital content delivered immediately, EU and UK law let you give up your
14-day right to withdraw in exchange for getting the file straight away. The
checkbox at checkout is that waiver. Without it we would have to make you wait
out the 14 days before sending the file, which is not what anyone wants.

If something is wrong with the file itself — it will not open, it is the wrong
book, pages are missing — that is a fault, not a change of mind, and you are
entitled to a fix or your money back regardless of the waiver.

## Refunds

Write to help@example.com. A refund cancels the download links.

## Complaints and disputes

Write to us first. If that does not settle it, EU consumers can use the
[Online Dispute Resolution platform](https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr/).
